XMX Brand Mark — Usage Guide

Matched to existing product laser engrave & marketing: thin geometric sans, open tracking, no ornament.

Primary wordmark

XMX
XMX
XMX

Construction: four characters as a single mark XMX. X = two sharp diagonals crossing; M = verticals + sharp center V (not rounded). Weight ≈ light / thin. Tracking ≈ 0.35–0.45em. Never bold condensed.

On-product scale

XMX
XMX

Charging case front: center, subtle deboss or tone-on-tone. Stem: micro laser, same proportions.

Do

  • Use open letter-spacing
  • Keep sharp corners on X
  • Tone-on-tone on metal/champagne shells
  • White on charcoal marketing
  • Clear space ≥ height of “X” on all sides

Don’t

  • Stretch or outline the mark
  • Add drop shadows / neon gradients
  • Replace with heavy black logo fonts
  • Write “xmx” lowercase as primary
  • Crowd logo against icons

Factory marking note (中英)

Please laser-engrave or pad-print the wordmark XMX using a light geometric sans, wide tracking, sharp X terminals. Do not use Arial Black / Impact / rounded logos. Minimum height on case: 3.5 mm. Depth of laser: subtle, matching champagne / black finish samples.

外殼絲印／鐳射字標請使用細線幾何無襯線「XMX」，字距偏寬，X 字尖角清晰； 禁止粗黑體。充電盒正面居中，顏色與殼同色系暗紋或淺灰白（視底色）。
